# Local tool preview design

16:9, 1920 × 1080, 16 seconds, silent. Dark charcoal #181917 canvas; white text; orange #ff994e accents derived from the screenshots. Arial/Helvetica system font, 58 px headings, 29 px body, 24 px persistent disclosure. Two static screenshot showcases with short opacity fades; no simulated clicks, Gmail activity, typing, or deletion.

Use the original screenshots without pixel alteration, contained at their original aspect ratios. Query screenshot uses a landscape panel; storage screenshot retains its narrow viewport. Synthetic labels are clearly separate from the captured UI. Do not imply account storage was measured, messages were matched, or actions were performed.
