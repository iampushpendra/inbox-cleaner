How to Delete Instagram Emails in Gmail

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: To delete Instagram emails in Gmail, search from:mail.instagram.com -is:starred, tick the top checkbox, click "Select all conversations that match this search," and hit Trash. Nearly every Instagram email, from "you have unseen notifications" reminders to new follower alerts, comes from a mail.instagram.com address. Then turn off email notifications in Instagram's settings so they stop coming back.

Instagram is one of the noisiest senders in a typical Gmail account. It emails you when someone follows you, when a friend posts for the first time in a while, when you have unread DMs, and, most often, when you simply haven't opened the app in a few days. None of these emails hold anything you can't see in the app. They just take up space and push real mail down the list.

How do I delete Instagram emails in Gmail?

Instagram sends almost everything from addresses ending in mail.instagram.com. One search catches the bulk of it. This version keeps security alerts out so you can check them first:

  1. In Gmail's search bar, type from:mail.instagram.com -from:security@mail.instagram.com -is:starred and press Enter.
  2. Tick the checkbox at the top-left of the message list. That selects the 50 emails on the current page.
  3. A banner appears: "Select all conversations that match this search." Click it. Now every matching Instagram email is selected, not just the visible page.
  4. Click the Trash icon. Everything moves to Trash and stays recoverable for 30 days.
  5. Run a second search for from:instagram.com -is:starred to catch mail from plain instagram.com addresses. Trash those the same way.

Trashed emails still count toward your 15 GB of storage until Gmail removes them. If you need the space now, open Trash and click Empty Trash now. Clearing other networks too? Which Instagram emails are safe to delete?

Almost all of them. Before you clear everything, it helps to know what each sender does:

  • no-reply@mail.instagram.com: follower alerts, likes, comments, unread DM nudges, and "see what you missed" reminders. Safe to delete.
  • security@mail.instagram.com: login codes, new-device alerts, and password resets. Old ones are safe to delete. Check recent ones first.
  • instagram.com addresses: product news, tips, and some account notices. Safe to delete unless you're waiting on a support case.
  • Meta Accounts Center mail: if you linked Instagram to Facebook, some account emails arrive from facebookmail.com. Search those separately so you don't sweep up Facebook mail you meant to keep.

Search operators stack, so you can be precise. To keep the last two weeks and clear everything older, search from:mail.instagram.com older_than:14d. To clear only security emails older than three months, search from:security@mail.instagram.com older_than:3m. If you run a business or creator account, keep emails about payouts, branded content, or account verification, and star those before you start.

What if Gmail won't delete all my Instagram emails at once?

The "select all conversations" banner works well for a few thousand emails. Above that, it often stalls, deletes only part of the selection, or times out. Then you're back to 50 emails per page. Ten thousand Instagram reminders means 200 rounds of select and delete.

Gmail also won't tell you how much of your inbox Instagram actually fills. You only find out by searching each sender by hand.

How do I stop Instagram from sending me emails?

Clearing the backlog does nothing about tomorrow's emails. Instagram will fill your inbox again within weeks unless you change its settings. You can do this in the app:

  1. Open Instagram, go to your profile, and tap the menu icon in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Settings and activity, then Notifications.
  3. Scroll down and tap Email notifications.
  4. Turn off Feedback emails, Reminder emails, Product emails, and News emails. Reminder emails are the big one. They drive most of the "you have new notifications" mail.
  5. Leave Support emails on. Instagram doesn't let you turn these off anyway, and they cover security and account issues.

Menu names shift between app versions. If you can't find the setting, search "email" in Instagram's settings. For marketing emails from other services that ignore your preferences, see How to Unsubscribe from Emails in Gmail.

Can I make Gmail delete Instagram emails automatically?

Yes. If you still want some Instagram alerts but don't want them piling up, set up a Gmail filter. In the search bar, type from:mail.instagram.com -from:security@mail.instagram.com, click the search options icon, and choose Create filter. Tick Delete it, and also tick Also apply filter to matching conversations to clear the existing backlog in the same step.

Prefer to skip them without deleting? Choose Skip the Inbox (Archive it) instead. The emails stay searchable but never interrupt you. For more filter recipes and how to avoid catching the wrong mail, see Gmail Filters to Delete Emails Automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting Instagram emails in Gmail affect my Instagram account?

No. The emails are only copies of alerts. Your posts, DMs, followers, and in-app notifications stay on Instagram exactly as they were.

What email address does Instagram use?

Most Instagram emails come from addresses ending in mail.instagram.com, such as no-reply@mail.instagram.com and security@mail.instagram.com. Some account and support mail comes from instagram.com or facebookmail.com through Meta's Accounts Center.

Why does Instagram keep emailing me about notifications I already saw?

Instagram sends reminder emails when you haven't opened the app recently, even if you saw the activity elsewhere. Turning off Reminder emails in Instagram's email notification settings stops them.

Is it safe to delete Instagram login code and password reset emails?

Yes, once they are a few days old. Login codes expire within minutes and reset links within hours. Keep recent security alerts until you've checked them, since they warn you about sign-ins you didn't make.

Can I recover Instagram emails I deleted by mistake?

Yes, within 30 days. Open Gmail's Trash, search from:instagram, select the emails you need, and click Move to Inbox. After 30 days, or after you empty Trash, they are gone for good.
