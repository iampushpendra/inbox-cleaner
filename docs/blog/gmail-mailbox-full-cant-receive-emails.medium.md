# Gmail Mailbox Full and Cannot Receive Emails? What to Check

If Gmail reports that storage is full, check the storage breakdown for the affected Google account first. Reduce usage in the service using the space, then test whether mail can be received. Do not assume deleted mail releases quota immediately or that previously bounced messages will arrive automatically.

## What should I do first when a full mailbox stops receiving mail?

1. Confirm the affected Google account, then open its Google One storage breakdown. Identify whether Gmail, Drive or Photos is consuming the space.
2. Review unwanted items in the service using the space. In Gmail, a search such as `larger:10M` can help identify candidates; inspect them before deleting.
3. Review Gmail Trash and Spam. If you choose to empty Trash, understand that this permanently removes its contents rather than preserving a recovery window.
4. Recheck the quota display and test delivery from another account. Ask senders of bounced important messages to resend after delivery works.

Google’s [storage policy](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9312312?hl=en) explains the shared quota and its effect on Gmail. Personal accounts include up to 15 GB; paid plans and Workspace accounts can have different limits. Use the actual quota displayed for your account rather than assuming everyone has the same allowance.

## What does the storage breakdown tell me?

| What you find | Useful next step | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail is the main contributor | Review large messages and unneeded mail. | Deleting blindly by age or sender. |
| Drive or Photos dominates | Review that service’s storage tools. | Assuming an empty Inbox means free account storage. |
| The displayed account is wrong | Switch to the affected account and check again. | Buying space or deleting mail in another account. |
| Usage changed but the display has not | Allow for storage processing and recheck. | Repeatedly deleting without confirming quota updates. |

## Frequently asked questions

**Does an empty Inbox mean the account has available storage?**

No. Archived mail, other Gmail folders and other Google services can still use account storage.

**Will buying a plan fix delivery immediately?**

Do not assume that. Google says additional storage can take up to 24 hours to become available; check the affected account and test mail afterward.

**Can sender counts tell me how much storage to recover?**

No. Counts measure mail volume, not message bytes or account quota. Diagnose storage separately.
