Gmail Mailbox Full and Cannot Receive Emails? What to Check

Updated 18 October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: If Gmail reports that storage is full, check the storage breakdown for the affected Google account first. Reduce usage in the service using the space, then test whether mail can be received. Do not assume deleted mail releases quota immediately or that previously bounced messages will arrive automatically.

What should I do first when a full mailbox stops receiving mail?

  1. Confirm the affected Google account, then open its Google One storage breakdown. Identify whether Gmail, Drive or Photos is consuming the space.
  2. Review unwanted items in the service using the space. In Gmail, a search such as larger:10M can help identify candidates; inspect them before deleting.
  3. Review Gmail Trash and Spam. If you choose to empty Trash, understand that this permanently removes its contents rather than preserving a recovery window.
  4. Recheck the quota display and test delivery from another account. Ask senders of bounced important messages to resend after delivery works.

Google’s storage policy explains the shared quota and its effect on Gmail. Personal accounts include up to 15 GB; paid plans and Workspace accounts can have different limits. Use the actual quota displayed for your account rather than assuming everyone has the same allowance.

Why am I still blocked after cleaning up?

Google’s storage guidance allows 48–72 hours for totals to update after deleting a large number of files. A storage-plan upgrade can take up to 24 hours to become available, so purchasing more space is not an instant-delivery guarantee. Read Google’s cleanup and upgrade troubleshooting.

Check whether mail remains in Trash and whether the shared account is still above quota. Use size-based diagnosis rather than guessing which senders occupy the most bytes. If your quota is sufficient but delivery still fails, follow Gmail troubleshooting or contact your Workspace administrator; storage is not the only possible cause.

Will emails that bounced arrive after I free space?

Do not rely on it. Once delivery works, ask the sender to resend important messages that failed. Keep any bounce details so the sender or administrator can investigate the actual error. A successful new test message does not prove that an earlier message was delivered.

Is every cleanup tool’s delete action recoverable?

No. Read the confirmation for the surface you use. Ordinary Gmail deletion moves mail to Trash, while Google One’s storage manager can offer permanent deletion for Gmail items. These are different operations. Check Google One’s deletion steps.

For a wider review before making a storage decision, see upgrading storage versus cleaning up.

Can I clear space from the Gmail app on my phone?

You can review and delete from the app, but that does not tell you which service is consuming account storage. Google documents deleting on Android by touching and holding a message, tapping the box next to “Select all” — which selects up to 50 messages by default, with further batches added by scrolling to the top and tapping it again — and then tapping Delete. Deleted mail moves to Trash and keeps counting toward usage until that Trash is emptied, so treat the app as a review surface and recheck the storage breakdown afterwards. Follow Google’s Android deletion and recovery steps rather than assuming the desktop controls exist. Those steps cover Android; the iPhone and iPad apps have their own controls and were not reviewed here. Inbox Cleaner requires desktop Chrome.

When does sender cleanup make sense instead?

Inbox Cleaner is an option when you want to clear mail from selected senders. It runs inside an already-open Gmail tab in desktop Chrome and groups discovered senders by email count. Counts are exact for the top 250 senders when resolved, and approximate counts use a tilde (~); counts are not storage measurements.

The extension moves selected senders’ mail to Trash. It does not carry over your date, size, category or starred-mail exclusions. Stay with native Gmail when those limits matter, and check which account is open before starting. Sender selection and the Move to Trash confirmation are separate actions.

Frequently asked questions

Does an empty Inbox mean the account has available storage?

No. Archived mail, other Gmail folders and other Google services can still use account storage.

Will buying a plan fix delivery immediately?

Do not assume that. Google says additional storage can take up to 24 hours to become available; check the affected account and test mail afterward.

Can sender counts tell me how much storage to recover?

No. Counts measure mail volume, not message bytes or account quota. Diagnose storage separately.
