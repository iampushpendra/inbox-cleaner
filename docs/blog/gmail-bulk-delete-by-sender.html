How to Bulk Delete Gmail Emails by Sender

Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: To bulk delete Gmail emails by sender, connect your account at Inbox Cleaner, which ranks every sender in your inbox by email count. Check the boxes next to the senders you want gone — you can pick several at once — and click Move to Trash to clear all of their emails in a single action.

Most cluttered inboxes aren't random — they're dominated by a handful of repeat senders: a retailer that emails daily, a social network's notification address, a newsletter you signed up for once. Deleting by sender is the fastest way to make a real dent, but Gmail doesn't make it easy to see who's actually flooding your inbox, let alone clear several senders in one pass.

How do I bulk delete Gmail emails by sender?

The fastest approach starts by figuring out which senders are worth targeting, then clearing them in bulk:

  1. Go to inbox-cleaner.vercel.app and click Connect Gmail.
  2. Inbox Cleaner scans your inbox and lists every sender, ranked from most emails to fewest.
  3. Check the box next to each sender you want cleared out — you're not limited to picking just one.
  4. Click Move to Trash. Every email from every selected sender moves at once.

This is different from deleting all emails from one sender in Gmail's native search bar, which only handles one address at a time and still runs into the 50-emails-per-page selection limit once you hit "select all."

How do I find out which senders are flooding my inbox?

Most people don't actually know which senders are worth deleting until they see the numbers. Gmail's search bar can tell you the count for one sender at a time — search from:sender@example.com and check the result count — but doing that address by address is tedious and you have to already know who to check. A ranked list flips that: instead of guessing, you see every sender sorted by volume, so the biggest offenders are obvious in seconds.

Can I delete emails from multiple senders at once in Gmail?

Not with Gmail's native tools in a single action. You'd need to search for one sender, select all, delete, then repeat the entire process for the next sender — and each round still caps at 50 selected emails per page unless you use "select all conversations that match this search." Doing this for five or six senders back to back is exactly the kind of repetitive task that makes inbox cleanup feel endless. Selecting multiple senders in one ranked list and deleting them together turns a multi-step chore into one click.

How do I bulk delete by sender using Gmail search operators?

If you'd rather stay inside Gmail and only need to clear one or two senders, search operators work fine for smaller volumes:

  • from:sender@example.com — everything from one exact address
  • from:(sender1@example.com OR sender2@example.com) — combine a few senders in one search
  • from:example.com — every address at a given domain

Run the search, click the checkbox, then "Select all conversations that match this search," and delete. It works, but combining more than two or three senders in one query gets unwieldy fast, and you're still bound by the same per-page selection limits covered in how to bulk delete Gmail emails.

Is bulk deleting by sender safer than deleting everything at once?

Yes, generally. Deleting by sender lets you make deliberate choices — clear out the retailer that emails you daily while leaving your bank and your family untouched. A blanket "delete everything older than X" approach is faster but riskier, since it doesn't distinguish between a newsletter you don't care about and a receipt you might need later. Ranking by sender gives you the context to decide sender by sender instead of guessing at a date cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

Does bulk deleting by sender delete emails permanently?

No. Emails move to Trash first, where they sit for 30 days before Gmail permanently removes them. You can restore anything from Trash during that window.

Can I bulk delete by sender without installing anything?

Yes. Inbox Cleaner runs entirely in your browser tab — there's no extension or app to install. You connect your Gmail account, review the ranked sender list, and delete directly.

What if I only want to delete some emails from a sender, not all?

Bulk-delete-by-sender tools remove every email from a selected sender. If you need to keep specific messages, star them first or manually exclude the ones you want to keep before bulk deleting the rest.

How do I stop new emails from a sender after bulk deleting?

Deleting clears the backlog but doesn't block future mail. Unsubscribe using the link in the email, or block the sender from Gmail's message menu, then set up a filter so anything that slips through gets auto-deleted.

Is there a limit to how many senders I can select at once?

No practical limit. You can check as many senders as you want in the ranked list before clicking Move to Trash — the tool processes them all in one pass through the Gmail API.
