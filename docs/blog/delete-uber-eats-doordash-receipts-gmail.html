How to Delete Uber Eats and DoorDash Receipt Emails in Gmail

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: To delete Uber Eats and DoorDash receipt emails in Gmail, search from:doordash.com or from:uber.com "Uber Eats", click the top checkbox, choose "Select all conversations that match this search," and click Trash. Add older_than:3m -is:starred to keep recent orders and any receipts you need for expenses. If you have thousands of them, Inbox Cleaner clears every delivery sender in one click.

Food delivery apps are relentless emailers. Every order sends a confirmation, often a "your order is on the way" update, and a receipt. Then come the promos: "$0 delivery fee this weekend," "Your favorites are back," "Rate your order." Order dinner twice a week for three years and you're looking at well over a thousand emails from Uber Eats and DoorDash alone.

You almost never need these emails. Both apps store your full order history and receipts in your account. The inbox copies are just clutter, and they're easy to clear once you know the right search.

How do I delete Uber Eats and DoorDash receipt emails in Gmail?

  1. In the Gmail search bar, type from:doordash.com for DoorDash, or from:uber.com "Uber Eats" for Uber Eats, and press Enter.
  2. Add older_than:3m -is:starred to the search. This keeps your last three months of orders (useful for refund disputes) and skips any receipts you've starred.
  3. Click the checkbox at the top of the message list. Gmail selects the 50 emails on screen.
  4. Click the banner that says "Select all conversations that match this search" to extend the selection to every match.
  5. Click the Trash icon. The receipts move to Trash and stay recoverable for 30 days.
  6. Repeat for the other app, so both services are cleared.

Want to do both in one pass? Use from:doordash.com OR (from:uber.com "Uber Eats") older_than:3m -is:starred. If you also use Grubhub, Postmates, or Instacart, add OR from:grubhub.com OR from:instacart.com. For more ways to stack operators like these, see Gmail Search Operators to Find Old Emails.

Why does from:uber.com catch my Uber ride receipts too?

Uber runs rides and Uber Eats from the same domain. Ride receipts, Eats receipts, and Uber One membership emails all arrive from uber.com addresses, often the same noreply@uber.com sender. A bare from:uber.com search will sweep up all of it.

That's fine if you want everything gone. If you need ride receipts for work travel, add "Uber Eats" to your search so only food orders match. You can also search from:uber.com subject:trip first to see what ride emails you have before deciding.

Which delivery receipts should I keep before deleting?

Most are safe to delete because the apps keep the real record. Uber Eats stores orders under Orders in the app, and DoorDash keeps them in the Orders tab. You can reopen a past order and view the receipt there.

A few are worth a second look first:

  • Work meals you expense. If your company reimburses meals, forward those receipts to your expense tool or star them. Then the -is:starred filter protects them.
  • Business tax deductions. Freelancers and small-business owners who deduct meals should export receipts before clearing the emails.
  • Open refund or missing-item claims. Keep anything tied to a dispute until it's resolved. The older_than:3m filter covers most of these.
  • Gift card emails. DoorDash and Uber gift cards sometimes arrive by email. Search gift card within those senders before you delete.

What if Gmail won't delete all my delivery emails at once?

Gmail's "Select all conversations" link works on small batches. On a few thousand emails it can stall, time out, or delete only part of the selection, and you're left running the same search over and over. It's the same limit described in How to Delete All Emails from One Sender in Gmail.

How do I stop Uber Eats and DoorDash emails from piling up again?

Your next order restarts the pile, so set up two things after the cleanup.

Turn off marketing emails in each app. In DoorDash, open Account, then Notifications, and switch off promotional emails. In Uber Eats, open Account, then Settings or Privacy, and turn off marketing communications. Receipts keep coming because they're transactional, but the deal alerts stop.

Create a Gmail filter for receipts. Search from:doordash.com, click the filter icon in the search bar, then choose Create filter. Pick Skip the Inbox and apply a label like "Receipts" so they're archived and searchable. If you never look at them, choose Delete it instead. Do the same for from:uber.com "Uber Eats". For more recipes, read Gmail Filters to Delete Emails Automatically.

Filters only act on new mail. To keep old receipts in check, re-run your older_than:3m search every few months and clear what's built up.

Should I archive delivery receipts instead of deleting them?

Archiving hides emails from your inbox but keeps them in All Mail, so they still count against your 15 GB of Google storage. Delivery receipts are image-heavy, and the promo emails that come with them are even heavier. If you're short on space, delete. If you like searching Gmail for "what did I order last Tuesday," archive with a label. The full tradeoff is in Gmail Archive vs. Delete: What's the Difference?

Frequently asked questions

Can I still get an Uber Eats receipt after deleting the email?

Yes. Open the Uber Eats app, go to Orders, tap the past order, and choose View receipt. You can also find past trips and orders at riders.uber.com and download receipts from there.

Where do I find old DoorDash receipts if I delete the emails?

Open the DoorDash app or website, go to the Orders tab, and select the order. The receipt and order details are stored in your DoorDash account, not just in Gmail.

Will deleting Uber Eats emails also delete my Uber ride receipts?

It can. Uber Eats and Uber rides both send from uber.com addresses, so a plain from:uber.com search catches both. Add "Uber Eats" to the search to target only food orders.

How do I stop DoorDash promotional emails but keep receipts?

In the DoorDash app, open Account, then Notifications, and turn off marketing emails. Order receipts are transactional, so DoorDash keeps sending them, but the promo offers stop.

Are food delivery receipt emails taking up a lot of Gmail storage?

Each one is small, but they're image-heavy and frequent. A few orders a week over several years can add up to thousands of emails and tens of megabytes, plus the promo emails that come with them.
