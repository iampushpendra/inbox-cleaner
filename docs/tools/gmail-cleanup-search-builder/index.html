Add criteria to begin.

      Check the account and scope in Gmail. This tool cannot choose your account. A standard Gmail search excludes Spam and Trash; in:anywhere includes them. It does not mean “Inbox only.”

      Opens a new tab at mail.google.com. This tool only searches; it never selects, archives, or deletes mail. Google may save searches under your account settings.

      From a search to a careful review

      1. Narrow the search. Choose the sender, category, age, dates, or size that describe the messages you want to inspect.
      2. Check and read the query. Resolve errors and read the cautions. The checker does not run a Gmail search or estimate result counts.
      3. Review in Gmail. Verify the active account, inspect individual messages and conversation contents, and adjust the search if the results are broader than expected.

      Synthetic examples

      These examples are illustrative, not recommendations to delete their results.

      from:newsletter@example.com older_than:6m

      A sender combined with a relative age. The date threshold moves over time.

      category:promotions before:2025/01/01 larger:10M

      A category, fixed before date, and Gmail’s message-size notation combined.

      Why the checker cannot promise a safe selection

      Gmail matches messages first. A conversation can appear even when some messages in it do not meet an exclusion such as -is:starred. Exclusions are not whole-thread protection. The local checker cannot inspect your mailbox, interpret every Gmail operator, or guarantee matches or deletion safety.

      Can I share or save my query here?

      This tool does not create query links, read criteria from URL parameters, or save inputs. Copy the query if you need to keep it, remembering that sender addresses and search terms can be personal.
