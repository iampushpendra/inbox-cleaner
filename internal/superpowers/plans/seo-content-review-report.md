## What changed

- Replaced fixed50 desktop-limit explanations, measured-looking scan/deletion timings, all-mailbox guarantees, mobile-extension claims and unqualified exact-count claims. Native all-results selection is separate from current-page selection.
- Preserved the distinction between matching messages and displayed conversations. Negative/starred queries are not blanket protection for starred messages in mixed threads. No financial or legal retention age is prescribed.
- Reframed bank, payment, receipt and invite guides around record review. Actual sender placeholders are explicitly examples, not claims that a hard-coded domain captures all mail safely. No promise that old records remain available from a third-party account.
- Substantively refreshed the seven pending briefs: one sender, Archive versus Delete, Promotions, bulk deletion, unread mail, old mail and unsubscribe. Gmail Manage subscriptions is acknowledged; blocking routes mail to Spam rather than preventing attempted delivery. Age filters are not rolling deletion timers.
- Removed stale FAQ/HowTo blocks on the 44 rewritten articles; retained truthful Article metadata instead of maintaining schema for removed or overstated answers. Existing generator-owned related sections were left for the controller to regenerate from current titles.
- Each rewritten page has a direct answer, focused native job details, concise conversation/recovery caveats and relevant next steps. A dedicated product-evidence guide is linked rather than repeating a long generic product pitch. Financial, invite, filter, Spam and permanent-removal guides identify sender-wide extension cleanup as a poor fit when scope must stay narrow.
- Homepage now leads with the desktop Chrome installation and open-Gmail requirement. Five authored journeys connect selection, senders, storage, old records and future subscriptions to relevant guides/tools. Visible FAQs and FAQ schema use the same answers. The optional legacy OAuth app remains usable, clearly labelled separately; its scan-speed claim was removed.
- Privacy separates DOM extension storage/permissions, the legacy OAuth/API app, local tools, website hosting/resources and the explicit Apps Script waitlist submission. The source does not establish waitlist backend retention, so no invented schedule is promised.
- Store copy is an unpublished draft with scope/count/recovery limitations, all three permission explanations and unverified installed/dashboard state. No store submission was performed.

## Why retain rather than automatically consolidate or noindex?

The saved Search Console snapshot under `research_notes/Analytics intelligence 2026-10-17/` and the refresh briefs provide disclosed query/page evidence, but not current post-refresh outcomes or a complete backlink/conversion comparison. Existing impressions/clicks are not proof of content quality, nor is topical overlap alone enough to redirect an established URL. all 49 pages now have an explicit retain/review disposition below. No noindex, redirect or consolidation has been silently inferred from missing live evidence.

Potential overlap remains in the broad cleanup family (clean-up, best-way, five-minute, inbox-zero), mass/bulk/thousands family, and storage family. Their editorial distinctions are now stated: goal selection versus staged inbox organization, bounded-batch planning versus selection execution, and capacity planning versus full-storage troubleshooting. This pass does not claim those distinctions have been validated by search results. After recrawl, compare query-to-page overlap, clicks, impressions, conversions and backlinks before deciding whether to consolidate. Any redirect decision needs a separately reviewed target and migration plan.
