Why Is Gmail Storage Full When My Inbox Is Empty?

Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: Gmail storage can be full while your inbox is empty because the inbox is only one view of your mail. Archived mail in All Mail, the Promotions, Social and Updates tabs, Sent, Spam and Trash all count, and so do Google Drive, Google Photos and backups, which share the same Google Account storage. Check the account's storage breakdown first, then search in:anywhere larger:10M in Gmail on a computer to find large mail wherever it is.

An empty inbox means no messages carry the Inbox label right now. It doesn't mean the account holds no mail. Archiving, filters, category tabs and sending all leave messages in your mailbox without showing them in the inbox. Google's storage help says Gmail messages and attachments count toward your quota, including Spam and Trash. The same page says that quota, up to 15 GB on a standard account, is shared with Drive and Photos.

Gmail documents the tab, archive, label and search behavior in its inbox categories help, archive help, labels help and search operator reference. A label isn't a second copy. Gmail's labels help says deleting an email removes it from every label and your inbox. Removing a label doesn't delete the message either.

How do I find what is using the space?

  1. Read the usage breakdown. Open the Google storage page for the account that shows the warning. Check usage by service. If Drive, Photos or backups use most of the space, deleting email won't fix much.
  2. Find large mail anywhere in Gmail. On a computer, search in:anywhere larger:10M. Google documents in:anywhere as including Spam and Trash, so results can include mail outside the inbox and mail you already deleted.
  3. Check archived mail. Search in:archive, or in:archive larger:10M, to see what left the inbox but stayed in All Mail.
  4. Check mail you sent. Search from:me has:attachment larger:10M. Files you attached to sent mail stay in your account too.
  5. Review Spam and Trash. Open both from More in the left menu. Google says items there count toward storage until you permanently clear them.
  6. Delete, then empty Trash. Move what you don't need to Trash. When you're sure, empty Trash. Google says storage normally takes up to 48–72 hours to update after a large deletion.

Search results show whole conversations, but Gmail matches individual messages. A large result can be one message in a thread you otherwise want to keep, so open it before deleting. Google's storage cleanup help suggests has:attachment larger:10M for the same job. Deleting Gmail emails by size covers size searches in more detail. Before a large delete, read how to select all emails in Gmail, because the select box covers one page until you use the select-all banner.

Why didn't archiving or inbox zero free any space?

Archiving takes a message out of the inbox. Gmail's archive help says you can still find it under All Mail. Inbox zero done with Archive leaves the mail where it was for storage purposes. Only deleting a message, then removing it from Trash, takes it out of your quota. Gmail archive vs. delete explains when each one fits. If storage is still full after cleanup, or mail is bouncing, see what to do when your Gmail mailbox is full.

When does a sender cleanup help?

Sometimes most of your archived mail comes from a few newsletters, stores or notification senders. In that case, removing mail by sender can clear years of messages your inbox never showed you. Gmail's own method is a from: search plus the select-all banner. How to delete all emails from one sender in Gmail walks through it.

Inbox Cleaner is a Chrome extension that groups Gmail mail by sender in desktop Chrome, with Gmail open in a tab. It moves the senders you choose to Trash. It shows message counts, not storage size, so a sender with many small emails may free less space than one large attachment. A sender-wide cleanup can't be limited by date, size, category or starred status. Mail it moves still counts until Trash is emptied or the 30-day period ends. If the breakdown shows Drive or Photos as the main user, start there instead.

Frequently asked questions

Does a Gmail label make a copy of an email?

No. A label is a tag on a message, not a separate copy in a folder. Gmail's help says that when you delete an email, it is removed from every label and your inbox, so adding or removing labels is not a way to change storage.

Does archiving emails free up Gmail storage?

No. Archiving removes a message from the inbox, but Gmail keeps it under All Mail. It still counts toward storage until it is deleted and removed from Trash.

Can mail in the Promotions or Social tab fill my storage if Primary is empty?

Yes. With the Default inbox type, Gmail sorts mail into Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums tabs. An empty Primary tab says nothing about the other tabs, and all Gmail messages count toward storage.
