How to Bulk Delete Emails in the Gmail App on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: To bulk delete emails in the Gmail app on iPhone, touch and hold one email, tap Select all at the top left, then tap Delete. Gmail selects up to 50 messages at a time by default, so for more you scroll back to the top and tap Select all again before deleting. Search first so every batch contains only the mail you mean to remove; deleted mail stays in Trash for up to 30 days.

This guide covers Google's Gmail app for iPhone and iPad. It doesn't cover Apple's built-in Mail app, which has its own controls. The steps below follow Google's iPhone & iPad deletion help, checked on 21 October 2026. App layouts can shift between versions, so compare the labels with your screen.

How do I select and delete many emails at once in the Gmail iPhone app?

  1. Narrow the list first. Tap Search in mail and type a search, for example from:news@example.com or category:promotions older_than:1y. Google says to use a search operator to delete specific emails, and the search operator reference lists the options.
  2. Start selecting. Touch and hold any email in the results.
  3. Tap Select all. It's at the top left. By default Gmail selects up to 50 messages.
  4. Add more if you need to. Scroll back to the top and tap Select all again to add another 50. Check the selected count at the top before you go on.
  5. Tap Delete. The emails move to Trash.

To delete just a few, touch and hold each message you want, then tap Delete. If you'd rather keep mail but clear it from the inbox, read Gmail archive vs delete before choosing.

Why don't other guides' steps match my Gmail app?

Many results for this search mix up two apps. Some guides tell you to tap Edit and then Select All. Google's Gmail app help doesn't use an Edit button; it uses touch-and-hold plus a Select all box. Several top results for this search also cover Apple's Mail app, so an Edit-first guide may be describing that app instead. Work out which app you're in before following any steps.

What should I check before tapping Delete?

Gmail search matches individual messages but shows whole conversations. Google notes that with negative operators, "conversations with excluded criteria may still appear", and the same applies to date cutoffs. A thread from older_than:1y can still hold a reply from last week. Before each Delete, open anything that looks important. Remember that Trash keeps messages for up to 30 days, after which Google deletes them permanently.

When should I switch to a computer?

The iPhone app is fine for a few batches. For thousands of emails, Gmail on a computer can select every match at once with the banner. If you want to clear out whole senders, Inbox Cleaner, the Chrome extension this site publishes, groups an open Gmail tab by sender and moves the senders you pick to Trash. It runs only in desktop Chrome, not on iPhone. It also can't keep recent, starred or category-specific mail from a sender it removes, so use a native Gmail search when you need those exceptions. Can I make deleting single emails faster with a swipe?

Yes. In the Gmail app, open the menu and tap Settings, then Inbox customizations, then Mail swipe actions. Choose the left or right swipe and set it to Trash. That suits daily triage. For backlogs, Select all is more practical.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Select all in the Gmail iPhone app stop at 50?

Google documents that the app selects up to 50 messages by default. To add 50 more, scroll back to the top and tap Select all again before tapping Delete.

Is there a Select all conversations that match this search option on iPhone?

Google's iPhone and iPad deletion help doesn't describe one. That banner is documented for Gmail on a computer, where it extends the selection beyond the current page.

Can I get bulk-deleted emails back on my iPhone?

Yes, for up to 30 days. Open the menu, tap Trash, touch and hold the messages, tap More, then Move, and choose Inbox or another label. Deleted drafts can't be recovered from Trash.

Does deleting in the iPhone app free Gmail storage right away?

Mail in Trash is deleted permanently after 30 days. To remove it sooner, open Trash and tap Empty trash now. After that the messages can't be recovered.
