How to Bulk Delete Emails in the Gmail App on Android

Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: To bulk delete emails in the Gmail app on Android, touch and hold one email, tap the box next to Select all at the top left, then tap Delete. Gmail selects up to 50 messages at a time by default, so for more you scroll to the top and tap Select all again before deleting. Search first so every batch holds only the mail you mean to remove; deleted mail stays in Trash for up to 30 days.

This guide covers Google's Gmail app on Android phones and tablets. It doesn't cover Samsung Email or other mail apps that can also show a Gmail account. The steps follow Google's Android deletion help, checked on 21 October 2026. App layouts can shift between versions, so compare the labels with your screen.

How do I select and delete many emails at once in the Gmail Android app?

  1. Narrow the list first. Tap the search bar and type a search, for example from:news@example.com or category:promotions before:2025/01/01. Google's Android help suggests search operators such as from:, after:, before: and has:attachment for deleting specific emails, and the search operator reference lists the rest.
  2. Start selecting. Touch and hold any email in the results.
  3. Tap Select all. It's a box at the top left. By default Gmail selects up to 50 messages.
  4. Add more if you need to. Scroll to the top and tap the Select all box again to add another 50. Check the selected count at the top before you go on.
  5. Tap Delete. It's at the top. The emails move to Trash.

To delete just a few, touch and hold each message you want, then tap Delete. If you'd rather keep mail but clear it from the inbox, read Gmail archive vs delete before choosing.

Can I delete all emails at once in the Gmail Android app?

Not in one tap, according to Google's Android help. There is no documented "delete everything" button. What you can do is open the view you want to clear and work through it in 50-message rounds:

Why don't other guides' steps match my phone?

Search results for this question disagree. Some say to tap the sender's picture to select, some say the app has no Select all at all, and some describe a different mail app. Google's current Android help documents touch and hold plus a Select all box with a 50-message default. If your screen shows neither, check that you're in the Gmail app rather than Samsung Email or another app, and that the app is up to date. We haven't reviewed other Android mail apps, so this guide makes no claims about their controls.

What should I check before tapping Delete?

Gmail search matches individual messages but shows whole conversations. Google notes that with negative operators, "conversations with excluded criteria may still appear", because Gmail identifies matching messages first. The same logic applies to date cutoffs: a thread from before:2025/01/01 can still hold a reply from last week. Before each Delete, open anything that looks important. Trash keeps messages for up to 30 days, after which Google deletes them permanently. More search recipes are in Gmail search operators to find old emails.

When should I switch to a computer?

The Android app is fine for a few batches. For thousands of emails, Gmail on a computer can select every match at once with the notice link. If you want to clear out whole senders, Inbox Cleaner, the Chrome extension this site publishes, groups an open Gmail tab by sender and moves the senders you pick to Trash. It runs only in desktop Chrome, not in Chrome on Android or the Gmail app. It also can't keep recent, starred or category-specific mail from a sender it removes, so use a native Gmail search when you need those exceptions. Can I make deleting single emails faster with a swipe?

Yes. In the Gmail app, tap Menu, then Settings, then General settings, then Swipe actions. Next to Right swipe or Left swipe, tap Change and choose Delete. That suits daily triage. For backlogs, Select all is more practical.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Select all in the Gmail Android app stop at 50?

Google documents that the app selects up to 50 messages by default. To select 50 more, scroll to the top and tap the Select all box again before tapping Delete.

Is there a Select all conversations that match this search option on Android?

Google's Android deletion help doesn't describe one. That banner is documented for Gmail on a computer, where it extends the selection beyond the current page.

Can I get bulk-deleted emails back on Android?

Yes, for up to 30 days. Tap Menu, open Trash, touch and hold the messages, tap More at the top right, then Move to, and choose your inbox or another label. Deleted drafts can't be recovered from Trash.

Does deleting in the Android app free Gmail storage right away?

Mail in Trash is deleted permanently after 30 days. To remove it sooner, tap Menu, open Trash and tap Empty trash now. After that the messages can't be recovered.
