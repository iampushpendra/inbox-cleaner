How to Select Multiple Emails in the Gmail App

Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: To select multiple emails in the Gmail app on Android or iPhone, touch and hold one email (or tap the sender's picture next to it), then touch and hold each other email you want. To grab a batch, tap the box next to Select all at the top left. Gmail selects up to 50 messages by default, and you tap it again to add more. The app doesn't document a way to select every email matching a search at once; that needs Gmail on a computer.

This guide covers Google's Gmail app on Android phones and tablets and on iPhone and iPad. The selection steps are the same on both. What changes is where some actions sit after you've selected, so there's a side-by-side table below. Everything here comes from Google's own Gmail help, checked on 23 October 2026. App layouts can shift between versions, so compare the labels with your screen.

How do I select more than one email in the Gmail app?

  1. Narrow the list first (optional). If you only want certain emails, search before selecting, for example from:news@example.com or is:unread. The search operator reference lists the options.
  2. Start selecting. Touch and hold an email. Google's help also describes tapping the sender's profile image on the left of the email instead.
  3. Add emails one by one. Google's help says to touch and hold each email you want. A check mark shows which ones are in.
  4. Or tap Select all. It's a box at the top left. By default Gmail selects up to 50 messages. Tap the box again to add the next batch.
  5. Check the count, then act. Look at the number at the top before you tap anything. Then choose an action from the top bar or from More.

Should I touch and hold or tap the sender's picture?

Either. Google's help pages describe both ways in, on both Android and iPhone, but on different pages. Its delete, archive and label pages say to touch and hold. Its mark as read page says to tap the sender's profile image. That's why guides online disagree about which gesture is "right".

The same pages also disagree on adding the next batch of 50. The delete page says to scroll back to the top and tap Select all again. The mark as read page says to scroll down, then tap it again. Don't rely on either: check the selected count at the top after each tap.

Delete, archive and mark as read sit in the top bar on both. Labels and moving sit under More, and the labels differ by platform.

Can I select every email that matches a search in the app?

Not in one step, as far as Google's help describes. In the app, Select all adds up to 50 messages at a time by default, so a long list means repeating it. Gmail on a computer is different. After you select a page, Gmail shows a link such as "Select all 2,000 conversations in Inbox" that selects every match. How to select all emails in Gmail walks through the computer version, and why select all conversations isn't showing covers what to check when that link doesn't appear.

One caution if you searched first: Gmail search matches individual messages but shows whole conversations. Google's search operator page notes that with negative operators, "conversations with excluded criteria may still appear". Open anything that looks important before you delete or archive the batch.

When is the phone the wrong tool?

The app is fine for picking a few emails or a few batches. If you're selecting the same sender's mail over and over, a computer is usually easier. Inbox Cleaner, the Chrome extension this site publishes, groups an open Gmail tab in desktop Chrome by sender and moves the senders you pick to Trash. It doesn't run in the Gmail app. It also removes everything from a chosen sender, so it can't keep recent, starred or category-specific mail. For those exceptions, use a Gmail search instead, as described in how to delete all emails from one sender in Gmail.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see Select all in the Gmail app?

In Google's steps, the Select all box appears at the top left only after you have selected one email by touching and holding it or tapping the sender's picture. If it still doesn't appear, check that you're in Google's Gmail app rather than another mail app, and that the app is up to date.

Can I mark all emails as read in the Gmail app?

Google's app help describes doing it in batches: tap a sender's picture, tap Select all, repeat to add more, then tap Mark as read. It doesn't describe a single action for every email in a label. Gmail on a computer can select every matching conversation at once.

Does Inbox Cleaner work in the Gmail app?

No. Inbox Cleaner is a Chrome extension that runs in an open Gmail tab in desktop Chrome. On a phone, use the Gmail app's own selection tools.
