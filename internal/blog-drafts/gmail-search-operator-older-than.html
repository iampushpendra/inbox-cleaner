How to Use the older_than Search Operator in Gmail

Updated October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: To use the older_than search operator in Gmail, type older_than: followed by a whole number and a unit, d (day), m (month) or y (year), with no spaces: for example older_than:1y. Add other operators after a space to narrow it, such as category:promotions older_than:1y, or pair it with newer_than: to search an age band. Review the results before you act. A returned conversation can contain newer messages.

This guide covers the operator itself: how to write it and how to combine it with Gmail's other operators. Every operator below comes from Google's Gmail search operators reference. If you only need the cleanup steps for one cutoff, start with how to delete Gmail emails older than a year. For ready-made old-mail recipes, see Gmail search operators for reviewing old emails.

How do I write an older_than search?

The operator has three parts: older_than:, a number and a unit letter. Google lists three units and gives older_than:1y and newer_than:2d as its examples.

You want mail older thanTypeNote
3 daysolder_than:3dDays use d.
2 weeksolder_than:14dThere is no documented week unit, so convert to days.
6 monthsolder_than:6mm means month, not minute.
18 monthsolder_than:18mUse a smaller unit instead of a decimal such as 1.5y.
1 yearolder_than:1yA rolling age. The set changes from day to day.

Google's reference says a space or a dash separates a number in a query, so keep the number and unit together. If a search returns nothing or the wrong mail, compare what you typed with these examples before changing anything else.

How do I combine older_than with other operators?

This is where older_than becomes useful. Google documents AND, OR or braces { }, the minus sign - for exclusions, and parentheses ( ) for grouping. The table shows each one with an age condition. The sender addresses are placeholders. Copy the real address from a message in your mailbox.

JobQueryWhat it matches
Old mail in one categorycategory:promotions older_than:6mPromotions messages older than six months. Requires inbox categories.
An age bandolder_than:1y newer_than:2yMessages between one and two years old.
Either of two senders, both old{from:a@example.com from:b@example.com} older_than:1yOld mail from either sender. The braces mean OR.
Old mail, except one senderolder_than:1y -from:boss@example.comOld mail without that sender. See the mixed-conversation caveat below.
Old mail under a labellabel:receipts older_than:2yLabelled messages older than two years.
Old mail including Spam and Trashin:anywhere older_than:1yGoogle documents in:anywhere as including Spam and Trash.
Old and largeolder_than:1y larger:10MOld messages over 10 MB. Results are not sorted by size.

Two rules make these easier to get right. A plain space between conditions means all of them must match, so the age band needs both halves. And put OR alternatives inside braces, so the age condition applies to every alternative rather than to only one of them.

How do I build and check a combined search?

  1. Start with the age alone. In Gmail on a computer, search older_than:1y. Go to the last page of results and check that the dates are old.
  2. Add one condition. Add a single narrowing condition, such as category:promotions, and rerun the search. If the results vanish, the condition you just added is the cause.
  3. Add exclusions or alternatives. Add a minus exclusion or a braces group last, once the core search returns what you expect.
  4. Review before acting. Open any result that shows a recent date before you select or delete anything.

When you are ready to act on a large result set, see how to select all emails in Gmail for selecting beyond the current page.

Why does an older_than search show recent conversations?

Google explains that Gmail "identifies matching messages first", then shows their conversations. It gives this as the reason a search with excluded criteria can still return conversations that contain them. The same applies to age. If a 2023 message matches older_than:1y and someone replied last week, the whole conversation appears with last week's date. Deleting it removes the recent reply as well. The minus exclusion has the same limit. -from:boss@example.com does not keep that sender's messages out of a mixed conversation. Open conversations like this and handle individual messages when the newer ones matter.

When is older_than the wrong tool?

Use before: when you need a fixed calendar date that will not move, such as before:2025/01/01. Google documents dates in the form 2004/04/16 and 04/16/2004. And don't put an age condition in a filter as if it were a timer. Filters test each email as it arrives, and new mail is never a year old. See what Gmail filters can do automatically.

Sometimes age doesn't matter and the goal is clearing out a few heavy senders. Inbox Cleaner runs in an open Gmail tab in desktop Chrome, groups senders by email count, and moves the selected senders' emails to Trash. That action covers all of a sender's mail. It does not keep an older_than cutoff, so the sender's recent mail goes too. Counts are email counts, not storage size. If the age limit matters, use the native search. For the sender-only approach, see deleting all emails from one sender in Gmail. Mail in Trash is normally recoverable for up to 30 days, as described in Google's deletion and recovery help.

Does older_than work in the Gmail app?

Google's reference walks through the steps for Gmail on a computer. It doesn't say whether the Android or iPhone apps handle each operator the same way. If a combined search behaves unexpectedly on your phone, repeat it in Gmail on a computer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find emails between one and two years old in Gmail?

Search older_than:1y newer_than:2y. Both conditions must match, so the results are mail older than one year but newer than two years.

Can I use weeks or hours with older_than?

Google's reference lists only d (day), m (month) and y (year). For two weeks, use older_than:14d.

Is older_than:12m the same as older_than:1y?

Google does not document how months and years are converted. Expect the two to be close, and check messages near the boundary if the exact cutoff matters.

Does older_than search Spam and Trash?

Not unless you widen the scope. Add in:anywhere, which Google documents as including Spam and Trash.
